Data & sources: Sun/Earth geometry from an hourly NASA/JPL SPICE DE440 MOON_ME vector table, with observer position correction, checkable against NASA/JPL Horizons. Terrain uses measured NASA LOLA elevations (16 × 16 km, 33 m display grid; 20–80 m source near the pole) from NASA Goddard PGDA. Site coordinates: DLR Connecting Ridge study, LROC; ≈ marks approximate values. Font: Inter (SIL OFL). Data derivatives and exact coverage: provenance. Catalogue site horizons include terrain to 300 km. Published PSRs are used directly. Vehicle capabilities and suitability thresholds are explicit scenario assumptions. Geometric visibility is not panel output or a certified radio link.

Positions use hourly SPICE vectors for 2024–2036 UTC (DE440, MOON_ME). Live monthly Horizons verification status is shown above; archival checks alone do not certify a full month. Azimuth is clockwise from north; longitudes are east-positive. Sunlight is not panel power, Earth in view is not a guaranteed radio link, and this is not a landing-safety assessment.
